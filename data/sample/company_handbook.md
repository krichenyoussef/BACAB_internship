# Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

This document is a fictional example used to demonstrate the chatbot. Northwind Labs is not a real company.

## 1. Working hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.
Employees may start between 8:00 and 10:00 as long as they work 7.5 hours per day.
Remote work is allowed up to two days per week, after approval by the team lead.

## 2. Leave

- Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; they expire on 31 March.
- Sick leave requires a medical certificate from the third consecutive day of absence.

## 3. Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission.
Receipts are mandatory for any expense above 20 EUR.
Travel must be booked through the internal travel tool; economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 35 EUR per day.

## 4. IT and security

Laptops are provided by the IT department and must be encrypted.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and changed every 6 months.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to it-support within 24 hours.
Multi-factor authentication is required for email and all internal tools.

## 5. Training

Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,000 EUR.
Training requests are approved by the team lead and must relate to the employee's role.
Employees can also spend 4 hours per month on self-directed learning during working hours.
